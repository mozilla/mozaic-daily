## Adjustments

### Headwind (`h`)
`data-official/2026-06/adjustments/headwind.json`:

```json
{
  "type": "linear_ramp",
  "start_date": "2026-04-01",
  "anchor_date": "2026-12-15",
  "desktop_dau": -1420000,
  "mobile_dau": -27162
}
```

Desktop anchor **-1,420,000** sized to land Dec-15 28dMA within ±5k of April's. April's was -1,497,870; the ~5.2% addback reflects Prophet absorbing some of the predicted Win10 headwind into its trend during Apr–May actuals. Mobile anchor unchanged from earlier cycles.

### Marketing-lift (`m`, mobile only)
`data-official/2026-06/marketing/marketing.json` points at `marketing_lift_model.real_data_v2.hybrid.2026-05-22.parquet` (v2 hybrid, training_end=2026-05-20). v2 has a peak-then-decline shape (peak ~early-July) → year-end mobile lift materially smaller than the earlier v1 model.
