1. The Dec-15 KPIs

OKNo moving holiday enters either KPI window, and the window holds the same holidays in 2026 and 2027

The DAU KPI is the 28-day average ending Dec 15 (Nov 18 → Dec 15). Both years hold US Thanksgiving on Thursday (Nov 26 2026, Nov 25 2027) and Black Friday, and nothing else of size. The model removes -320K/day on average inside the 2027 window, against -331K in 2026: a 11K year-over-year difference. The MAU KPI (raw Dec-15 MAU) carries -10K of holiday effect in 2027 and 0K in 2026.

One modelling fault does touch this window (section 4): the 2025 outage label diluted what the model learned from Thanksgiving 2025. Removing it would deepen the window by -70K in 2027 and -79K in 2026, so it shifts both years equally and leaves the comparison alone. Thanksgiving Day itself is forecast at −27% against −29% to −34% actual (2022–2025); Black Friday's −31% matches history.

2. Watch list: where 2027 daily actuals will diverge from the forecast

Ranked by the people involved and by evidence that the model gets this kind of arrangement wrong. The evidence is a holdout test: holiday effects fit only on data before 2025-12-01, then scored against Dec 2025 → Oct 2026, which the model never saw. Over that whole span the predicted holiday effect was off by 161M person-days in absolute terms.

HIGHChristmas → New Year 2027: the biggest holiday period, on a weekday layout the model has seen once

Dec 25 2027 and Jan 1 2028 both fall on Saturday, so the US/ROW calendar puts "Christmas Day" on Fri Dec 24 and "New Year's Day" on Fri Dec 31 (observed days). Training has that layout only in 2021. In the holdout the model got the 2025/26 holiday hole 11% too shallow (-21M person-days over Dec 18 → Jan 10). The ROW and US late-December clusters remove 44M and 17M person-days in 2027. This falls after Dec 15, so the KPIs are unaffected; December tracking and the Jan-2027 MAU dip (the biggest MAU holiday effect of the year) are.

HIGHEuropean May: Ascension five days after May 1 has never appeared in training

Easter is early in 2027 (Mar 28), which pulls Ascension to Thu May 6, five days after Labour Day (Sat May 1). In 2021–2026 the gap was 8–28 days. France adds Victory Day on Sat May 8, and Poland adds its May 3 holiday. Whit Monday is May 17 and Corpus Christi is May 27. The 2026 holdout shows how the machinery handles a crowded May: the model's dip was 1.73× what happened in France, 1.40× in Germany, 2.11× in Poland and 1.21× in ROW. Whit Monday 2026 was forecast at the −60% floor in DE and FR against −38% and −32% realized, because it carries two labels (native + "Global") that are added together. 2027 person-days removed: DE 8.2M, FR 5.7M, PL 4.5M, ROW 12.4M (May 1–6 cluster). Expect May 2027 actuals to run above forecast, with the biggest misses on May 6–7 and May 17.

HIGHEid in the rest-of-world group: a real dip that the ROW calendar has no label for

ROW (33% of world DAU) uses the US calendar plus Christian and Global add-ons. It has no Islamic holidays, but it contains Türkiye, Egypt, Pakistan, Bangladesh, the Gulf and North Africa. Every year since 2023 ROW dropped 5–10% for 3–4 days at Eid al-Fitr. The model forecasts none of it: -5.4M person-days around Tue 2027-03-09, peaking at -1.6M on one day (3.1% of world DAU). Eid al-Adha (Sun 2027-05-16) is a -3.9M dip that the model gets by accident: the spurious Whit Monday label lands the next day (card below).

HIGHWhit Monday (Global) puts a dip on an ordinary Monday

Mozaic adds "Whit Monday (Global)" to every country, including the US, Canada, China, India, Iran and Japan. In 2026 it coincided with US Memorial Day (May 25), so the label learned Memorial Day's dip. The holdout forecast the US for May 20–30 at 2.2× what happened. In 2027 Whit Monday is May 17 and Memorial Day is May 31, so the label removes -4.2M people on May 17 across countries where it is not a holiday: US -14%, Canada -15% (-1.3M together, on a normal Monday), ROW -10%, Indonesia -29%. In ROW and Indonesia it lands the day after Eid al-Adha, so there it partly stands in for an Eid dip that is not modelled. Treat May 17 as a known false dip in US/CA.

MEDIUMEaster 2027 (Mar 28), Holi (Mar 22) and Good Friday

An early Easter is a familiar arrangement (2024 was Mar 31), but the 2026 holdout over-predicted it: Germany 1.17×, ROW 1.35×. Germany's 2027 cluster also holds Saint Joseph's Day (Mar 19, a mozaic add-on). In India, Holi (Mar 22) and Good Friday (Mar 26) fall 4 days apart, an arrangement seen in two training years; the holdout's March India window was 1.16×. Expect Mar 26–29 actuals slightly above forecast.

Not a collision, but the holdout under-predicted Spring Festival 2026 by 40% (realized -6.1M vs predicted -3.7M person-days). China is 2.9% of DAU. Expect February actuals in China below forecast.

3. Indonesia in detail

Indonesia (2.8% of world DAU, about 1.5M DAU) has Muslim, Christian, Hindu, Buddhist and Chinese holidays all on the national calendar, plus the add-ons mozaic gives every Paschal-cycle country (Mardi Gras, Palm Sunday, Corpus Christi, Assumption, All Saints', Whit Monday). Most of 2027's collisions live here.

MEDIUMLebaran (Eid al-Fitr, Tue–Wed Mar 9–10, with Nyepi on Mon Mar 8)

The biggest Indonesian event: desktop DAU falls 40–79% over roughly ten days. The 2027 model curve follows past years' shape well, but the −60% floor clips the −67% to −79% core days and the tail (days +6 to +9) comes out too shallow. History averages -5.65 Indonesia-days lost; the model has -4.47. That gap is about -1.8M person-days. In the holdout, Lebaran 2026 came out 27% too shallow. Ramadan starts ~Feb 8, two days after Lunar New Year (Feb 6). Islamic dates in the calendar are library estimates; the government's announced date can move them a day. The collective-leave days (cuti bersama) that stretch the break are not in the calendar.

MEDIUMFour Indonesian arrangements new in 2027

  • Jan 1 → Jan 5: Isra' Mi'raj falls four days after New Year's Day (it falls twice in 2027, the second time on Christmas).
  • May 16 → Jun 6: Eid al-Adha (Sun 16), Whit Monday (add-on, 17), Vesak (Thu 20), Corpus Christi (add-on, 27), Pancasila (Tue Jun 1) and Islamic New Year (Sun Jun 6) form one chain three weeks long. The comparable 2026 stretch came out 1.26× too deep in the holdout.
  • Aug 14 → 17: the Prophet's Birthday falls three days before Independence Day.
  • Dec 25: Christmas and Isra' Mi'raj on the same Saturday. The model adds both effects together and hits the −60% floor; a single day off will not drop twice.

4. How the model attributes holidays (why collisions confuse it)

Mozaic learns one effect per (holiday, days-from-holiday). A day within ±7 days of several holidays splits its dip among them by inverse distance (closer gets more), and the forecast adds up each holiday's learned effect at its 2027 offset. When 2027 puts holidays at spacings training never had, the sum is an extrapolation. Four specific faults make it worse:

MEDIUMTwo labels for the same day off get counted twice

When two labels share a row ("New Year's Day; New Year's Day (Global)", "Easter Sunday; Pasqua di Resurrezione"), the fit gives each label the full dip and the forecast adds them. Removing the duplicates changes 2027 DAU by 65M person-days (Jan 1 alone: -26.8M → -19.1M). But the holdout does not support removing them for DAU: absolute error 161M as built vs 163M without duplicates. Averaging across years pulls each effect down (2020 enters as a zero year, and 2022–23 dips were small), which roughly cancels the doubling. For MAU, removing them helps (92M → 79M). Where duplicates clearly hurt is a duplicated Monday (Whit Monday, above).

LOWThe 2025 Legacy-outage label dilutes every autumn-2025 holiday

The outage is labelled as a holiday on every day from Aug 16 to Dec 10 2025, in every country, so it took ~80% of the credit for Labor Day, Golden Week, Diwali, All Saints' and Thanksgiving 2025 (Thanksgiving learned −6% for 2025 against −29% realized). 2027's autumn holidays are therefore forecast 16M person-days too shallow in total. The holdout covers too little autumn to test the fix.

LOWSmaller items

  • The day-of-week scale is capped at 1.25 after it is applied (core.py _predict_holiday_effects), so the cap never acts; Monday holidays in DE/US/ROW use an uncapped multiplier.
  • Same-day stacks of two different holidays in 2027: Indonesia Dec 25 (above) and Iran Apr 2 (Martyrdom of Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq + Nature's Day).
  • Spurious add-ons with learned effects: Corpus Christi in the US and ROW (May 27, four days before Memorial Day), Mardi Gras and Corpus Christi in Indonesia, Whit Monday and Christmas Eve in China, India, Iran and Japan.
  • India in 2027: Prophet's Birthday, Independence Day and Raksha Bandhan within three days (Aug 14–17); Maha Shivaratri three days before Eid (Mar 6/9); Eid al-Adha four days before Buddha Purnima; Gandhi Jayanti a week before Dussehra. Each is new, but India's effects are small (1–3M person-days).

5. Every 2027 collision cluster

A cluster is a chain of 2027 holidays in one country, each within 7 days of the next (the window mozaic splits dips over), containing at least one moving holiday. Tier rule: High = a pair of real local holidays at a spacing never seen in 2021–2026 and ≥ 3M world person-days removed; Medium = a new real pair with ≥ 1M, or any new pair (including add-ons) with ≥ 3M; Low = everything else. This table ranks calendar novelty only; the watch-list cards above add the evidence that overrides it (ROW Christmas is "Low" here because no moving holiday is new, yet it is the biggest single period).

6. Method and reproduction

  • Model: data-official/2027-glean-draft/desktop_glean_history_cpr087_o_2026-10-10/…/mozaic_objects.glean_history_desktop.2026-10-10.pkl (LOCK.md build 5). Read-only; no forecast was rerun.
  • Every effect is computed with mozaic's own Mozaic._fit_holiday_effects / _predict_holiday_effects on the summed country tiles; the as-built variant reproduces every tile's stored 2027 effect exactly (asserted).
  • "Actual dip" in the Eid/Lebaran plots is model-free: observed ÷ mean of the same weekday 3–4 weeks before and 4–5 weeks after, minus 1. "Realized" in the holdout is mozaic's own target, observed ÷ detrended − 1.
  • Scripts (run from the repo root, in order): extract_holiday_effects.py, run_counterfactual_fits.py, run_holiday_backtest.py, then from research/holidays-2027/: build_holiday_review.py, render_holiday_review.py.
  • Scope: desktop only (the 2027 Glean-history build). The same calendar machinery drives mobile, so the mechanism findings carry over; the person-day numbers do not.
