## Reading the charts

- **The step down at the seam is expected, not a break.** The Win10 headwind is applied from the seam
  forward at its already-ramped value — the ramp starts 2026-04-01, so at 2026-07-28 it is already about
  −615K. A trailing 28-day MA cannot physically move that far in one day; the discontinuity is the
  display-layer adjustment switching on. July's charts show the same step at their own seam. See
  `research/ma-seam-turbulence/`.
- **The gold low/baseline/stretch markers are NOT August targets.** They are the June-cycle aspirational
  benchmarks, reused unchanged in July and again here purely so the vertical scale stays comparable
  across the three cycles' charts. No August target has been set.
- Y-axis ticks are formatted to two decimal places of a million so adjacent ticks stay distinguishable
  (a whole-million formatter would render 48.52M and 48.59M as the same "49M").

**Where new files go:** charts from this cycle's notebooks. Cross-month diagnostic plots belong under
