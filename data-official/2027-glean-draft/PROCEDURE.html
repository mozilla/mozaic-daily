2027 Glean-history desktop forecast

Goal, the decisions taken so far, what the data is, the procedure proposed, and what is still open. Companion to data-official/2027-glean-draft/_index.md.

1. Goal

Produce a draft forecast of Firefox Desktop MAU (headline) and Desktop DAU (secondary) through 2027-12-31, trained on the legacy-to-glean project's Glean-equivalent history as if it were real Glean history, because Glean desktop telemetry alone is too short to train on and Legacy telemetry shuts down at the end of 2026. The headline statistic is raw daily MAU on 2027-12-15; a 2026-12-15 value is produced too. This is a wholly separate exercise from the monthly refresh cycles: all head- and tailwinds are off in the first build and are re-added one at a time afterwards, with the combinatorics machinery ready for that. Mobile is not part of this; the normal cycle supplies it. Method rule from Brendan: our methods on their data.

3.2 Facts that shape the design

  • The export's Legacy input is mozaic-daily's Legacy input. World legacy_dau matches the sum of our legacy desktop pull to within 0.01% on every shared day 2020–2026. The translation is a slowly varying multiplier (28-day Glean/Legacy ratio 1.035–1.039 across 2020–2026) plus a constant-share Glean-only term.
  • Published Glean vs the headline. On DAU the world gap is small (within 0.2% through Jul 2026, −0.3/−0.5% in Aug/Sep 2026); the 2026-09-01 single-day +0.8% I first quoted is dominated by Linux temporary + day noise. On MAU the gap is real and time-varying: −7% in 2025 falling to −1.4% by Sep 2026, mostly duplicate-id inflation from pre-Fx-148 builds (see the plot's Windows MAU panel and §7).
  • No Windows-version split exists in the translation. Tiles must be OS × country.
  • The seam is a join rule, not applied in the file. Every row through 2026-09-08 is the translation. The export project's rule: translated through the seam (Win/Mac 2026-08-23), published Glean after, with a filter on Linux.
  • Iran's 2026 shutdown crater is untreated in the export; IR cells carry it raw.
  • Legacy outage windows are left in: Windows 2025-08-16..11-06, Mac 2025-08-28..12-10 (§4).
  • Trust map (export agent): own-factor and unflagged on both metrics: Windows × US BR CA DE IT, Mac × US CA DE FR JP MX. Pooled on both metrics: Windows × FR CN JP RU IR. Iran is pool-translated on all three OS (≈523K Windows DAU). Linux is mostly pooled and flagged, including Linux × US.
  • Known small negative bias of the delivered translation on the overlap test: DAU −0.14%, MAU −0.37% (accepted by the owner).

5. Procedure proposed

Each step names its artifact, its check, and the tests/docs it carries (per CLAUDE.md, plans include both).

Step 0 — Register a new data source, no BigQuery

  • Add DataSource.GLEAN_HISTORY_DESKTOP ("glean_history_desktop", short code gh) to queries.py, with no QuerySpec: it is file-fed. get_queries skips it; the pre-flight BigQuery check is bypassed for it.
  • Extend the desktop output contract: update_desktop_format is unchanged (segment JSON {"os": population}), validation.py gains windows / mac / linux as allowed os values for this data source only, so production validation is untouched.
  • Tests: tests/test_glean_history.py — the new source is excluded from BigQuery query building; validator accepts the new segments only under gh.

Step 1 — Input adapter: export → mozaic raw frames

  • New module src/mozaic_daily/glean_history.py: reads the two fc15 parquets, emits per metric a frame with columns x, country, windows, mac, linux, y (exactly one flag True per row; ROW rows from the export's {os}_ROW), glean_equivalent as y, dates ≥ 2020-01-01, and writes mozaic_parts.raw.glean_history.desktop.{DAU,MAU}.parquet plus a sidecar recording source sha1s and the column choice.
  • Metric name for the MAU track: "MAU" is not in the Metric enum (only Existing Engagement MAU). Add Metric.MAU (short code M) rather than mislabel the series.
  • Checks: per-OS sums reproduce {os}_total on every day; row count = 16 countries × 3 OS × days; no NaN.
  • Tests: adapter identity on a small fixture; one-hot invariant; ROW inclusion; date floor.

Step 2 — Seam and splice decision needed, §6 item 7

Facts from the export agent (§7): the history is the Fx 148–153 Glean state applied to every Legacy day; the Legacy input is available in BigQuery through 2026-10-10 (the table stopped at 09-08 only because that was the data end at build time); the SDK 68 step at the 2026-08-23 seam is −0.1 to −0.2 pp on DAU and MAU at OS level (Windows × US DAU: +0.01 pp), so no level correction is needed on Win/Mac DAU and at most −0.2 pp on MAU. Three ways to continue the series to early October, all on the headline's definition (published Glean minus temporary minus duplicate-id, never published verbatim):

  1. Top-up run of the translation in the export repo (agent's recommendation, same object as the history): apply the delivered lookups to Legacy days after 09-08, recompute the temporary and duplicate-id terms. About an afternoon of agent time, under 0.1 TB, needs your go-ahead in that repo (MAU read budget and stop rule).
  2. Cheap approximation per cell: published Glean × (1 − e(OS, month)) on MAU, published on DAU, minus the temporary term where a window is open; within ~0.3% for Win/Mac outside the Linux and FR/CN windows. Doable here with one BigQuery pull.
  3. No splice: forecast start 2026-09-09, training end 2026-09-08. Loses four weeks of data; simplest and cleanest provenance.

Whichever is chosen: horizon → 2027-12-31, seam-step diagnostic per OS, human go/no-go before adoption. The Fx 155 re-measurement (late October) may re-set the Win/Mac reference; the forecast would then be one version state behind, with a known offset of −0.4% Windows / −0.7% Mac at client level and ~0 on published.

Step 3 — Iran counterfactual fill for this layout

  • The packaged fill is keyed to legacy_desktop with modern_windows/winX columns and will not splice onto windows/mac/linux frames (mozaic's splice_fill requires identical columns). Regenerate with the generate_iran_fill.py method on the translated IR cells: train on IR before 2026-02-28, forecast the gap, harvest per-population; DAU window 2026-02-28..05-25, MAU window 2026-02-28..06-21 (the FILL_FORMAT_SPEC rule).
  • Pass it via mozaic's additional_fills (no package edit). Seam-scaling stays a human go/no-go.
  • Caveat to record: IR is pool-translated on every OS, so the fill is a counterfactual of a pooled translation.
  • Tests: fill frame honours the spec invariants (country IR, dense window, one-hot, non-negative).

Step 4 — Legacy outage masking open item §6

Two candidate mechanisms, recommendation first:

  1. Project-local holiday calendar registered in ADDITIONAL_HOLIDAYS for the new source: one window per OS (Windows 2025-08-16..11-06, Mac 2025-08-28..12-10), applied as the union. Holidays are per country, not per population, but holiday_smart measures the effect per tile and only acts when the dip exceeds holiday_threshold (−3.2%), so Windows and Linux tiles see little effect from the Mac-only part of the window. The calendar populates 2025 only, so it cannot leak into the forecast years. Cheap, matches "we already handle this with holidays".
  2. Counterfactual gap fill like Iran's, per OS. Heavier; justified only if (1) leaves a visible residual in the Mac trend.
  • Check: plot per-OS 2025 fitted trend with and without the mask. Tests: calendar dates lock.

Step 5 — The raw build

  • New runner scripts/run_glean_history_forecast.py: injects the adapter frames (no BigQuery), uses main(model_configs=...) with the October g01 lock (cps 0.1649, cpr 0.814, ncp 40, recent 17, sps 0.00825, regime multiplicative, thresh −0.032, radius 5/3, floor −0.6), all adjustments disabled, DAU and MAU, writes mozaic_daily_forecast.<seam>.gh-D+M.raw.parquet + sidecar + fitted pickles under desktop_glean_history_g01_<seam>/.
  • MAU note: mozaic treats the metric generically (no MAU special-casing in the package); the desktop model is a log-space Prophet fit with weekly seasonality, which a rolling-28 series lacks. First build runs it as-is; if the weekly term misbehaves on MAU, that is the one place a config fork is justified, and it will be reported before being tried.
  • Checks: training rows byte-equal to the adapter frames; ALL = Σ country tiles; 2026-12-15 and 2027-12-15 values per OS and world; seam kink score.

Step 6 — Outputs and plots

  • csv/glean2027_canonical_curves.csv: world + per-OS, DAU 28d MA (display_ma from the package) and daily MAU, actuals from 2020 and forecast to 2027-12-31; csv/glean2027_dec15_summary.csv for 2026-12-15 and 2027-12-15.
  • Plots in the canonical format (full range + zoom on the seam and on the horizon end), world and per OS, DRAFT watermark.
  • A review notebook glean2027_canonical.ipynb that reads the parquet and renders everything.

Step 7 — Intervals (combined)

  • Prophet predictive: mozaic_daily.intervals on the fitted pickle (1,000 world sample paths; for MAU the band is on the daily series, for DAU on the 28d MA).
  • Translation: the export's correlated band at the roll-up level (world and per OS; cells have bands, ROW does not, so the world band comes from the global_all row, held at its last value through the horizon).
  • Combination: half-widths added in quadrature (independent error sources), with both components also reported separately. The combination rule is a proposal; see §6.

Step 8 — Comparison forecasts

  • Legacy DAU (exists): October's combinatorics manifest already holds the no-overlay raw desktop run at seam 2026-10-05 with horizon 2027-12-31 — a free Legacy comparison once h is left off. Caveat: it uses the Windows-version layout and a 2020 Legacy history; the comparison is in Legacy units (×~1.036 to Glean-equivalent).
  • Legacy MAU (new): no cycle has ever forecast plain MAU. Needs one BigQuery pull (telemetry.active_users_aggregates, mau column, same grouping) and one run of the same runner on Legacy data. Approval item.
  • Real Glean (new, secondary): glean_telemetry.active_users_aggregates from 2025-05-22 (the clean overlap), DAU and MAU, same runner. Short history, so expect it to be weak; reported for completeness.

Step 9 — Combinatorics readiness

  • No adjustment is applied in build 1. Specs are gated by applies_to_forecast_start today; add a applies_to_data_source check so a cycle spec can never leak into gh runs and vice versa.
  • Display-layer codes (h, t, u) re-add trivially. Per-tile overlays (e i j l o) target the modern_windows segment; in the OS layout they would land on windows, which also contains older Windows. That mapping is a decision for when they come back, not now.
  • The ladder/combinatorics builders are keyed on seam + config + overlay fingerprints and should work unchanged once the runner is pluggable; verify with one two-code dry run when the time comes.

What the correction did and did not do:

  • CN × windows DAU is repaired. The October 2027 crash is gone (monthly mean 1.74M instead of 0.84M; the plain build's daily minimum was −1.49M on 2027-10-06, build 3's is +884K); CN windows 28d-MA 2027-12-15 is 2.31M.
  • Every other country's DAU moved down 1.5–8.8% at 2027-12-15 (IN −8.8%, IR −3.8%, ID −3.4%, US −1.5%). Mozaic reconciles top-down: removing the June 2026 step from the world series lowers the world trend Prophet extrapolates, and that is pushed into every tile. This is the mechanism CLAUDE.md records for mobile ("per-tile knobs cancel"), now seen on desktop. Part of the −887K world change is therefore legitimate (the step no longer inflates the world trend) and part is reallocation; the Legacy comparison (production applies o the same way) is the check.
  • CN × mac is still broken, and the correction does not reach it. The export's mac_cn cell also doubled in June 2026 (31K → 63K → 70K; pooled factors, mac_mid), so the migration is visible on Mac in the translated series even though production treats it as Windows-only. The CN mac tile's forecast goes negative in both builds (monthly mean −208K plain, −573K with o, on a 70K tile), and because mozaic clips only the world paths, it drags CN's total and the world by that amount in late 2027.
  • MAU is untouched. The per-tile overlay machinery subtracts from and adds back to DAU only (overlays.py, metric_key = Metric.DAU.value). The CN MAU tiles still carry the step and still swing 9M → 2M in late 2027. The headline metric did not get the correction.

The 15-month Prophet band is not usable, and that is not specific to this forecast

The world 90% half-width on the 28d MA at 2027-12-15 is 25.2M (build 3) / 27.7M (build 1). I measured the production October desktop pickle (data-official/2026-10/desktop_g01_2026-10-05/, Legacy, Windows-version layout) with the same code: half-width 1.0M at 2026-12-15 but 19.9M at 2027-12-15. The g01 configuration's sample paths diverge with horizon (40 changepoints, range 0.814, prior scale 0.1649, multiplicative regime); September's quoted ±1.97M was a three-month horizon. The CN tiles made ours worse, but even fixed it would be ~20M. For a 2027 target the Prophet predictive interval is not a meaningful uncertainty statement; the translation band is, and a backtest-based error (how far past 12–15-month forecasts missed) would be.
